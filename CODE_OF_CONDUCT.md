# Community Code of Conduct

Forge contributors are expected to help maintain a respectful, constructive, and welcoming project community.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- Discussing technical disagreements with care and focusing criticism on ideas or code.
- Making space for different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Giving feedback that is specific, relevant, and aimed at improving the project.
- Respecting the privacy and safety of other participants.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory conduct, deliberate intimidation, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Contact a maintainer privately with concerns; reports will be handled with discretion where practical.
